# versio-vny404-navigator

travelers guide for vintage spacecraft: a one-page reference for **vny404 versio**, acidclank’s lo-fi vinyl and cassette firmware for the versio platform. every knob by its number, dust, tone, wow and flutter and the compressor drawn out, ducking and bypass. firmware: https://acidclank.gumroad.com/l/VNY404

source: the acidclank vny404 versio manual v1.0.

part of the [versio starsystem](https://mellotom.github.io/versio-navigator/) in the [travelers guide welcome center](https://mellotom.github.io/travelers-guide/).

not affiliated with acidclank or noise engineering.
